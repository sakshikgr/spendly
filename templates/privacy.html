Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data We Collect

To run your Spendly account, we collect:

  • Account details you provide, such as your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as your browser type and the pages you visit, used to keep the service working.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly:

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To store, display and summarise your expenses.
  • To fix problems and understand how the app is used.

We do not sell your personal data or use your expense records for advertising.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data and expense records, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation.

Third Party Services

We may use trusted third parties for services such as hosting and fonts. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, on our behalf and only to deliver their service. We do not share your expense records with third parties for their own purposes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access, correct or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
